# Product

## Platform

web

## Users

The primary users are hiring managers and recruiters evaluating Raniel Garcia for senior software engineering roles. They need to understand his technical depth, professional experience, and ability to lead complex backend and cloud work, then contact him about a suitable opportunity.

Technical peers are a secondary audience for the architecture articles, project write-ups, and case studies.

## Product Purpose

This portfolio presents Raniel's experience and engineering judgment through projects, case studies, technical writing, work history, and a resume. Its primary success outcome is a credible evaluation that leads a hiring manager or recruiter to contact Raniel about a senior engineering role.

## Positioning

Raniel is a senior .NET and Azure engineer who turns complex domains into reliable, evolvable distributed systems. His work emphasizes explicit domain models, strong architectural boundaries, observability, and pragmatic decisions that keep production systems correct and adaptable.

## Operating Context

Visitors evaluate the portfolio through the home, about, experience, projects, case studies, architecture, blog, resume, uses, search, and contact surfaces. Projects and case studies provide concrete technical context; articles demonstrate how Raniel reasons and communicates; the resume and experience pages support role evaluation; contact and public professional profiles provide the next step.

Portfolio content is authored as MDX under `content/`, with structured frontmatter controlling listings, filtering, featured content, drafts, and metadata.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Publishes project write-ups, case studies, and categorized technical articles.
- Supports featured and draft content, category and tag archives, search, an XML feed, social metadata, a printable resume, and a contact form.
- Uses Raniel Garcia's name, `ranielgarcia.dev`, and `hello@ranielgarcia.dev` as the public identity and contact details.
- Focuses professional terminology on .NET, Azure, distributed systems, cloud architecture, domain modeling, APIs, messaging, event-driven workflows, and production reliability.
- Some employment history, recommendations, performance metrics, and project claims currently in the repository are placeholders or otherwise unverified. Their authenticity is an open decision.
- Future work must not present an unverified claim as verified evidence, invent additional proof, or silently turn placeholder content into a factual endorsement.

## Brand Commitments

The product is Raniel Garcia's personal professional portfolio. It presents him as a Senior Software Engineer based in Manila, Philippines, with a direct, technically precise, and pragmatic voice. Existing identity assets include `public/profile.png`, `public/profile-2.svg`, and `src/app/favicon.ico`.

## Evidence on Hand

- Project write-ups in `content/projects/`, including the Enrollify and portfolio entries.
- Case studies in `content/case-studies/`, including scheduling-engine and legacy-migration narratives.
- Technical articles in `content/blog/` covering Clean Architecture, CQRS, and SignalR.
- Enrollify screenshots and a product recording under `public/projects/enrollify/`.
- A profile image and alternate profile illustration under `public/`.

These are available content artifacts, not blanket verification of every claim they contain. Employment details, recommendations, metrics, and project outcomes must be verified by Raniel before future work treats them as public proof.

## Product Principles

1. Lead with credible engineering judgment, not a list of technologies.
2. Make complex systems understandable through concrete decisions, tradeoffs, and outcomes.
3. Prefer precise, pragmatic technical language over inflated claims or architecture ceremony.
4. Give hiring evaluators a clear path from evidence to role fit to contact.
5. Preserve trust by distinguishing verified experience from draft or placeholder content.
